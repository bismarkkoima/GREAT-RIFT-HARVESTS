About Great Rift Harvests

At Great Rift Harvests, we are passionate about providing the finest flower arrangements for every occasion. From weddings and birthdays to corporate events, our flowers are handpicked from the lush landscapes of the Great Rift Valley.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring nature’s beauty to your doorstep, with a wide variety of floral arrangements that cater to every taste and budget. We believe that flowers have the power to brighten up any space and make life’s moments even more special.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Great Rift Harvests started as a small flower farm in the heart of the Great Rift Valley. What began as a local venture has now grown into a leading online florist, offering fresh flowers delivered directly to our customers. We take pride in our strong commitment to sustainability and working closely with local farmers to ensure the highest quality blooms.

Why Choose Us?

  • Fresh, handpicked flowers directly from the Great Rift Valley
  • Customizable flower arrangements for all occasions
  • Fast and reliable delivery service
  • Dedicated customer support team
